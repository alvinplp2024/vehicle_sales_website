ABOUT US


A BRIEF HISTORY:


S'N'J Auto Dealers LTD was launched in Decemeber 2018, incorporated as S'N'J AUTO DEALERS LTD.
In 2019 we introduced Car Finance and Car Insurance. This way car buyers who were unable to pay the full amount for the cars managed to secure bank facility from our partner finance institutions and at the same time, car insurance became only a click away.
In 2020 we launched Car Specifications. For the first time, car buyers in Kenya were able to which car model was produced which year and the variations in different car packages and series.
In 2021, we launched Kenya’s first Used Car Price Guide. For the first time, a used car buyer or seller could see what was the actual market value of their vehicle, depending on the car’s model, variant, age, condition, mileage, city and whether they were transacting with a dealer or an individual. We offer prices for all the popular cars sold in Kenya.
We also provide consultation services to our clients on all aspects associated with cars.

S 'N' J Auto Dealers Limited is a car dealership incorporated in Kenya dealing with importation and sale of new and second-hand Motor Vehicles.
At S 'N' J, we pride ourselves in offering quality and carefully sourced Vehicles, guided by our core values and a burning desire to satisfy customer needs.
Vehicles in our dealership are sourced from Japan, Thailand, United Kingdom, Australia, South Africa and host of other countries to ensure variety of units to meet market demand.

VISION

OUR SERVICES


1. Direct sale of brand new, foreign used and locally used Vehicles to both individuals and corporates.
2. Imports on behalf - allowing customers to ship in their desired Vehicle without the hassle and logistical complexities involved.
3. A service center offering Motor Vehicle maintenance services, such as respray, panel beating, upgrades, accessories and parts.
4. Trade-ins to allow clients to conveniently upgrade from current Vehicle to a newer and fresher unit. The trade-in Vehicle then covers part of the purchase price for their desired car.
